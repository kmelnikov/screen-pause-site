How it works

You decide before.
Step away during.

  1. 01 · Before

    Choose when to begin

    Start a manual pause from 1 second to 60 minutes, or enable Auto Pause for repeating breaks. Scheduled pauses ask first unless you opt in to a start without confirmation.

  2. 02 · During

    Let the pause run

    Every display goes black. Normal input briefly reveals the remaining time; it does not dismiss the pause. You can add 1, 2 or 3 minutes.

  3. 03 · After

    Return to your work

    Control returns automatically and silently. Work timers resume. If you use emergency release, Auto Pause stays suspended until you resume it.

Choose whether the app asks first.

Auto Pause schedules your series. The separate Auto option starts a particular series without asking and must be enabled explicitly.

In Adaptive, Auto is unavailable: “Early” postpones this pause; “Right on time” or “Late” starts it. Before a meeting, suspend Auto Pause from the menu.

Three rhythms

Pick what suits
your workday.

Choose one mode for Auto Pause. It applies to every series; each keeps its own timings.

Fixed

I want a predictable schedule.

Repeat the Work interval and Pause duration you choose. A straightforward place to start.

For example: 20 minutes of work, then a 1-minute pause, every cycle.

Breathing

I prefer a little variation.

Work and Pause gently rise and fall together, up to 15% above and below your values. Each series keeps its own rhythm.

Your values are the center of the wave. This varies timing, not breathing exercises.

Adaptive

I want to adjust through feedback.

Answer whether a pause came early, on time or late, and how its length felt. Learning stays on your Mac.

Set Work to 20 minutes and Pause to 1 minute: future cycles may ask sooner or rest longer. Work stays at or below your maximum; Pause stays at or above your minimum. Early postpones only the current pause.

Why pay $19.99 for Screen Pause?

Screen Pause is for people who notice break reminders but keep working anyway. It helps you follow through on the pause you chose: once it starts, every display is covered until it ends (with system-wide input blocking in the Direct DMG edition, or active full-screen pause windows in the upcoming Mac App Store edition) and an emergency release remains available. Independent series coordinate short and long breaks, preserving progress and merging pauses that fall due together. $19.99 is a one-time purchase with no subscription.

Can I end a pause early?

Normal keyboard and mouse actions do not dismiss an active pause. Use Control–Option–Command–Escape for emergency release. That pause does not count as completed rest, and Auto Pause stays suspended until you resume it.

What access does the Mac app need?

Both editions require macOS 13 or later. The Direct DMG edition requires Accessibility and Input Monitoring permissions to suppress input system-wide during a pause. The upcoming Mac App Store edition runs in App Sandbox and requires neither Accessibility nor Input Monitoring. After installing either edition, run the 10-second test in Settings → Access before a longer break. Input contents are never stored.

Will it interrupt a presentation or recording?

When macOS reports active screen sharing or recording, a due automatic pause waits. After that activity ends, the app waits for a fresh five-second natural stop before asking. You can also suspend Auto Pause from the menu before a meeting.

Is the browser preview the full app?

No. It darkens only this webpage for ten seconds and you can exit with Escape. The installed Mac app covers every display (with system-wide input blocking in the Direct DMG edition, or standard full-screen pause windows in the Mac App Store edition). The preview does not install anything or request macOS permissions.

What does the research say about breaks?

Research supports making room for breaks, but does not establish one ideal schedule or test Screen Pause itself.

  • A field study of 42 data-entry workers found less eye and upper-body discomfort with extra breaks, while output was maintained. Galinsky et al., Ergonomics (2000) ↗
  • A meta-analysis found small improvements in vigor and fatigue; the overall performance effect was not statistically significant. Albulescu et al., PLOS ONE (2022) ↗
